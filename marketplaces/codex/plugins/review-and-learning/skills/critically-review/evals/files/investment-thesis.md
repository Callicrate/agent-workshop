# Investment Thesis: LedgerLeaf

LedgerLeaf offers AI-powered bookkeeping for small businesses. The company can capture 5% of the $40B bookkeeping market within two years because bookkeeping is a universal pain point and the product demo is compelling.

Comparable SaaS companies routinely reach $100M ARR quickly, so LedgerLeaf's path is straightforward. Since bookkeeping is repetitive, the product will be highly sticky and support costs should remain low.

The team only needs 2,000 customers paying $20,000 annually to cross $40M ARR, which is conservative given the market size. Marketing should be efficient because founders have strong product intuition and early users love the concept.

Regulatory risk is limited because finance software has existed for years, and competition is less relevant because incumbents are not AI-native.
