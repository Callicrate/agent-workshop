# Memo: End Remote Work to Restore Productivity

Remote work has reduced productivity by 30% across the company. In Q1, the enterprise sales pod closed 7 deals while fully remote, compared with 10 deals in Q4 when the team was in the office more often. This proves that remote work is the main driver of the slowdown.

Employees also say they feel less connected, which further confirms the problem. Although engineering teams shipped more features during the same period, that is not relevant because sales is the clearest measure of company productivity.

The only realistic options are to return to full-time office attendance or accept continued underperformance. Competitors that are serious about execution have already made this shift.

A full return-to-office mandate should therefore be implemented next month.
