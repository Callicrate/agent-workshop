# Review Report

Write for a reader, not a parser. Replace these prompts with prose; omit empty sections.

## Overall Assessment
- `proceed: evidence-grounded rationale`
- Use exactly one of `proceed:`, `revise:`, `stop:`, or `insufficient-evidence:`.
- Confidence: `high`, `medium`, or `low`

## Scope and Materials Reviewed
- Decision or reliance question, artifact version, inspected sources, and material coverage limits.

## How It Works and What It Depends On

- Load-bearing assumptions and the boundary where the conclusion stops holding.

## What Holds Up
- Evidence-backed properties to preserve, and the conditions under which they hold.
